What I do

Full pipeline, one operator.

01

Videography

Music videos, live sets and content shot handheld and solo — built for low light and real locations, no crew required.

02

Photography

Live coverage, portraits and promo stills for artists and small businesses that need images fast and consistent.

03

Graphic design

Cover art, promo assets and brand collateral, designed to hold up across social, print and press kits.

04

3D & compositing

Blender-built elements and AI-assisted composites — portals, neon, motion graphics — cut into real footage in post.

About

Lotasroom LLC

Lotasroom is an independent creative media production company — videography, photography, graphic design and 3D work for musicians, content creators and small businesses. Based in West Haven, CT, with an active presence filming nights across New York City.

Every project runs through one operator, start to finish — concept, shoot and post — so the look stays consistent from the first frame to the final export.

Ongoing production work for Outside The Ghetto (OTG) and independent artists.

BASED New England / NYC
FOCUS Musicians, creators & small business
APPROACH Solo shooter, full pipeline
POST DaVinci Resolve / Fusion
